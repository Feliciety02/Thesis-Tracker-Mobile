# Team workflow

Use **Samples** for fieldwork and RCPC hand-over, **Tasks** for assignments, and **Home** for a quick check. The sample label connects the leaf, its five photos, and the RCPC result.

## 1. Before collection

1. Fe Anne sets the **collection date for this batch** on Samples. The hand-over is due the following day.
2. Each member opens the Android app while online and checks that they are signed in. A sync count means changes or photos are still waiting.
3. Bring labels, the packaging RCPC approved, and a charged phone.

## 2. At the farm

1. Tap **New sample** for each separate leaf. Write its automatic `FA-###`, `RJ-###`, or `CP-###` label on the bag.
2. Take the five prompted views. The Android app keeps a named Gallery copy and queues each photo for Drive.
3. Cut, bag, and label the leaf. **Bagged** is recorded when you leave the final camera screen.
4. Check the sync count when signal returns. A photo is confirmed only when its sample says **Saved to Drive**.

## 3. At RCPC

1. Fe Anne opens **Samples → Submission list**, checks the labels and selected hand-over date, and prints or saves the list if needed.
2. After RCPC receives the leaves, mark the selected samples as submitted. This records the date for the whole team.
3. If the team entered the date later, choose the **actual hand-over date**. The list automatically selects samples photographed the previous day; adjust the checkboxes if needed. A missing date appears under **Check dates** until it is recorded. Only a recorded date after the next-day deadline appears under **Invalid**.

## 4. After RCPC returns results

1. Match each RCPC result to its sample label and enter the **RCPC validated class** in the sample log.
2. Use **Samples → Paper numbers** for the confirmed class counts and, when ready, export the validated photos for training.

## Daily check

- **Home:** look at grouped work that needs attention.
- **Tasks:** keep assignments and due dates current. Completing the hand-over task on the due day can record the planned batch; use the Submission list to correct or backfill dates.
- **Sync count:** wait for it to clear before uninstalling the app or clearing phone storage. If it says **need attention**, open Samples to read the upload error.
